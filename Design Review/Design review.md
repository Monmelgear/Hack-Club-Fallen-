## Mechanical fit

Body, wires, motor height, and blade flex need a separate check. The assembly contains 34 modeled objects; electronics, props, medkit, and harnesses are not a fully populated fit model. The claw pose is illustrative. Verify jaw travel, servo horn and rod, grip, release, camera retention, PCB standoffs, battery restraint and loaded center of gravity.

## Power and controls
The nominal 650 mAh x 80C label implies 52 A total. The ESC rating is per motor. Verify combined current on the exact motor/prop combination, voltage sag, and ratings of the XT30, wire and battery. Confirm loaded mass and thrust margin. A larger battery also changes weight, balance and tray fit.
Use the 4S 2550KV motor variant and verify the matching 5030 prop hardware. The older 4-inch props are not approved by this review. The 5V UBEC feeds the claw servo; check regulator voltage, servo stall draw and J7 polarity. Verify ELRS bind, switch, receiver failsafe, free 3.3V UART and Pico response with propellers removed. User is supplying claw firmware. The local repository snapshot's servo.cpp uses other pins and lacks a semicolon; it does not establish working firmware. Verify the actual release source.
